Science of Life

The Wisdom of Ayurveda

Ayurveda, from Ayu (life) and Veda (knowledge), is India's traditional science of life. These timeless principles from classical texts guide every treatment we offer.

Holistic Approach

समदोषः समाग्निश्च समधातुमलक्रियः।
प्रसन्नात्मेन्द्रियमनाः स्वस्थ इत्यभिधीयते॥

— Sushruta Samhita, Sutrasthana 15

A person whose doshas are balanced, whose digestive fire and tissues function well, whose elimination is regular, and whose soul, senses and mind are at peace, is truly healthy. Ayurveda heals the mind, body and spirit together, not just the symptoms.

Preventive Care

प्रयोजनं चास्य स्वस्थस्य स्वास्थ्यरक्षणम्।
आतुरस्य विकारप्रशमनं च॥

— Charaka Samhita, Sutrasthana 30/26

The purpose of Ayurveda is to protect the health of the healthy and to cure the diseases of the sick. Daily routines (Dinacharya), seasonal care (Ritucharya), proper sleep and stress management keep you healthy for life.

Importance of Consultation

यथा विषं यथा शस्त्रं यथाग्निरशनिर्यथा।
तथौषधमविज्ञातं विज्ञातममृतं यथा॥

— Charaka Samhita, Sutrasthana 1

A medicine taken without proper knowledge can harm like poison, a weapon or fire. Guided by an expert, the same medicine heals like nectar. Always consult a qualified Ayurvedic physician.
