Terms of Service

Last updated: 2 November 2026

These terms apply to the website hsdbayurveda.com and the online services of {{ clinic.clinic_name }}, Ahmedabad ("the Hospital", "we", "us"). By using the website, creating an account or booking an appointment, you agree to these terms.

1.About our services

The website lets you learn about our Ayurvedic treatments, book appointments, view your prescriptions, bills and therapy progress, add family members and upload medical documents.

Information on this website (treatments, shlokas, articles) is for general awareness only. It is not a diagnosis or a substitute for a consultation with our doctor. Treatment, medicines, diet advice (Pathya–Apathya) and therapies are decided only after a personal consultation and Nadi Pariksha.

2.Not for emergencies

This website is not meant for medical emergencies. In an emergency, call 112 or go to the nearest hospital immediately. Do not wait for a reply to a booking, message or feedback.

3.Your account

You log in with your email address and password. Your account may be created by you (Sign Up) or by the Hospital, in which case you receive an email link to set your own password. The link is valid for 48 hours.

Account Security

Hospital staff never ask for or see your password. Keep it private. You are responsible for activity under your account.

  • Please give correct details (name, phone, date of birth, etc.). Wrong details can lead to wrong records.
  • If you add family members, you confirm that you are allowed to share their details and book on their behalf. For a minor, a parent or guardian must manage the booking.

4.Appointments

Appointments are booked for available time slots, subject to the doctor's schedule.

You can cancel a pending appointment from your account up to 24 hours before the appointment time. Within 24 hours, please contact the Hospital.
If the doctor is on leave or unavailable, the Hospital may cancel or reschedule your appointment. You will see the reason in your account.
If you do not attend, the appointment may be marked as missed.

5.Fees and payments

Consultation, medicine and therapy charges are as per the Hospital's current rates, shown at the Hospital or on your bill.

Payments are made at the Hospital (cash, UPI or other modes accepted there). The website does not collect card or bank details.

  • Therapies may be billed per session or as a package, as agreed at the time of prescribing. Questions about refunds or package changes should be discussed with the Hospital.

6.Medicines and therapies

Take medicines only as prescribed, with the advised Anupana and timing (Sevan Kaal). Tell the doctor about allergies, pregnancy, other medicines or health conditions.

Results of Ayurvedic treatment differ from person to person. The Hospital does not guarantee a specific result or time to recover.

7.Documents you upload

You may upload reports and documents (PDF, JPG or PNG, up to 10 MB each) to help the doctor.

  • Upload only your own or your family members' documents. Do not upload anything unlawful or unrelated to treatment.

8.Feedback

Feedback helps us improve. We will not publish your name or feedback publicly without your permission.

9.Proper use

Do not misuse the website: no false bookings, no access to other people's accounts or data, no attempts to break or overload the system.

10.Limitation of liability

We try to keep the website accurate and available, but it may sometimes be unavailable or contain errors. To the extent allowed by law, the Hospital is not liable for losses caused by website downtime or by relying on general information on the website instead of a consultation.

11.Changes and suspension

We may update these terms or our services. We may suspend an account that is misused.

12.Governing law

These terms are governed by the laws of India. Courts in Ahmedabad, Gujarat have jurisdiction.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 November 2026

We respect your privacy. This policy explains what personal data we collect, why, and your rights under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (India).

1.Data we collect

  • Account and contact details: name, email, phone number, address, date of birth, gender, blood group, profile photo.
  • Family members you add: name, relation, phone, gender, date of birth, blood group.
  • Health and treatment records: appointments, consultation notes, diagnosis, prescriptions (medicines, dosage, Anupana, Sevan Kaal), diet and lifestyle advice, therapy sessions, and documents uploaded by you or the doctor.
  • Billing records: bills, amounts, payment mode and status. We do not store card or bank details.
  • Old records: details from the Hospital's earlier paper registers may be added to your digital record.
  • Feedback: name, contact details, rating and message.
  • Technical data: essential cookies needed for login and security. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

2.Why we use it

  • To book and manage appointments and send you account, password and appointment emails.
  • To provide treatment: prescriptions, therapies, follow-ups and medical history.
  • To prepare bills and keep financial records.
  • To improve our services using feedback.
  • To meet legal and medical record-keeping requirements.

3.Who can see your data

  • Only authorised Hospital staff, according to their role: doctors for treatment, billing staff for bills.
  • Service providers that help run the website (server hosting, email delivery), only as needed to provide the service.
  • Government or legal authorities, when required by law.

We never sell or rent your personal data.

4.How we protect it

  • The website uses HTTPS (encrypted connection).
  • Passwords are stored in encrypted (hashed) form. Staff cannot see them.
  • Access to records is limited by staff role, and data is regularly backed up.

5.How long we keep it

Medical and billing records are kept for as long as required by applicable medical and legal rules, and as long as needed for your continued care. Other data (such as feedback) is deleted when it is no longer needed.

6.Your rights

You can:

  • view and update your profile details from your account;
  • ask for a copy or summary of your personal data;
  • ask us to correct wrong or incomplete data;
  • ask us to delete data that is no longer needed (medical records required by law may be kept);
  • withdraw consent for optional uses;
  • nominate a person to exercise your rights in case of death or incapacity;
  • raise a complaint with our Grievance Officer, and if not resolved, with the Data Protection Board of India.

7.Children

Details of children under 18 are added and managed by a parent or guardian.

8.Changes to this policy

We may update this policy.
