ABOUT US – HSDB AYURVEDA

Dr. Mohit Agrawal

Practising Authentic Ayurveda Since 2011

At HSDB Ayurveda, we are dedicated to promoting holistic well-being through the timeless wisdom of authentic Ayurveda. Led by Dr. Mohit Agrawal, who has been practising Ayurveda since 2011, our approach focuses on understanding the individual and guiding them towards a balanced and healthier lifestyle.

Explore Our Treatments

Rooted in Traditional Ayurvedic Wisdom

Dr. Mohit Agrawal has received training in Nadi Vaidya under the traditional Guru–Shishya Parampara, preserving the time-honoured knowledge and practices of Ayurveda. His approach combines traditional Ayurvedic principles with personalised guidance to support overall health and well-being.

Experience in Chronic Health Conditions

With years of clinical experience, Dr. Agrawal focuses on supporting individuals dealing with chronic health conditions through an individualised Ayurvedic approach. Each consultation aims to understand the person's unique health concerns and provide guidance in accordance with traditional Ayurvedic principles.

Bringing Ayurveda into Everyday Life

We believe Ayurveda is more than a system of healthcare—it is a way of life. At HSDB Ayurveda, we help individuals incorporate Ayurvedic principles into their daily routines, including guidance on dinacharya (daily regimen), lifestyle practices, and healthy habits to encourage balance and long-term well-being.

Our Vision

Our vision is to make authentic Ayurvedic wisdom accessible and practical for modern life while remaining rooted in traditional knowledge, personalised care, and a holistic understanding of health.
